# Prisma — replacement after user feedback

The user rejected Destino as bland and requested another functional design: modern, tech, eye-catching, colorful. Prior answers settle task (GIS batch processing/correction) and code-first. The request is to try the interface directly; this proposal is not recorded as their final visual approval.

Impeccable concept-seed: ab5ec911, bolder reroll1. The six dealt systems were fused to the same native GIS workspace, judged on audience identification and product clarity:

- Exposure record (leader): its tonal discipline helps surfaces, but hatch layers/fractions add irrelevant interpretation. Declined on both axes under the pinned brief.
- Airport signage: clear action hierarchy, but too close to rejected Destino and its yellow wayfinding. Competitive on clarity; not the build.
- Vertical media feed: one spatial item can lead, but hides batch comparison. Declined on both axes; kept a stronger actual-map footprint.
- Centre-rail reference setting: could link references, but the reading density and serif apparatus conflict with a fast GIS operation. Declined; kept stable keyed references.
- Boarding pass/gate board: rows retain identity during status changes, but travel material is irrelevant. Competitive on clarity; kept stable row identity.
- Dark-first working surface: best identification with the requested tech feel and clearest colored action/selection/status roles. Wins both axes against the leader. Palette/material are translated to graphite/lime/cyan/coral/violet; navigation and controls stay native. No fake command prompt, console logs, progress, charts, or sci-fi framing.

The catalog board and hero were viewed. The hero is not a layout template: its marketing headline, kicker and invented pipeline are excluded. No shipping raster or generated comp is used. Color is a role system, not random decoration.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A modern spatial analysis workspace, with a broad real map and a horizontal review ledger. Color connects a selected address to its marker and evidence, while every status retains its text.

OWN-WORLD: Prisma. Graphite canvas #10141c, elevated regions #171c26, lime #cbff6a for primary actions, cyan #78e4ef for selection, coral #ff9b8c for review and failures, violet #b7aaff for provenance. Space Grotesk headings, IBM Plex Sans controls, mono only for quantities/coordinates. The dark presentation is requested visual direction; no ambient-light or ergonomics claim is made. Standard web navigation, file input, table, buttons and details remain standard.

STORY: Import a file, confirm columns, process, isolate uncertain rows, inspect sources, correct coordinates and export selected points. The importer collapses after processing on mobile; export error and completion feedback are outside the disclosure.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: import region at left, broad map above a full-width ledger; evidence beside the map after data, and beside the introduction before data. Table controls share a compact band with enough body height to show rows. Mobile: import, table, map and evidence; editing/configuration stays accessible in the mobile disclosure. Required empty and filled captures at 1440, 390 and the actual user width 1304px (height884).

FORM: Impeccable seed ab5ec911, user-steered bolder reroll1. Challenger digital-design-canon-dark-first-developer-console wins against the exposure-record leader on audience identification and product clarity under the pinned tech brief. It contributes type, graphite elevation, role-based accents and density, not terminal syntax, fake charts or tool costume. Signature: the same cyan numbered reference appears on selected row, marker and inspector. Selection settles in180ms; reduced motion is immediate. OSM raster colors are transformed only at presentation; Mapa claro restores the original unfiltered tile colors, preserving attribution and geometry.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
